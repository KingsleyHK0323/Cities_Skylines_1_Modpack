- In an explicitly authorized disposable environment, establish the loading, provider, scheduler, unload and release behavior needed for an ordinary new city, including risks from duplicate, delayed, overlapping and replaced extensions. Consider a separate loaded city, scenario or editor only as needed to establish fail-closed behavior or support an explicitly authorized claim. A local event number or matching wrapper is not proof of the originating city.
- Establish a trustworthy current-city and provider identity, a generation boundary, and a way to reject stale or unregistered instances. The currently inspected callbacks do not provide authenticated city identity, so observing a few clean runs or all registered releases cannot establish this property. Keep submission disabled if the boundary cannot be proved.
- Verify scheduler submission, inline versus queued callback, cancellation, unload and exception ordering. Independently authenticate the actual simulation thread and complete pause state, including forced pause. `QueueSimulationThread` returning and the ordinary pause flag being false are insufficient. The inspected singleton lookup can create objects; it is not an established side-effect-free authentication method.
- If the optional diagnostic candidate is chosen for a live trace, establish a user-visible, default-off control and safe Unity UI-thread and panel-lifetime handling before using it. The candidate currently has neither activation nor display. Its fixed event codes and local labels can still encode identifying information; do not upload them or assume other game components cannot observe UI text. Other forms of authorized observation do not require this UI.

## Area state and native gates

- With separate approval for in-memory city-state reads in a disposable ordinary new city, check that each raw x and z is independently in 0–4, that the expected 25 distinct coordinates map to the inspected native indices, and that the area count and two passes agree. Retain only approved pass, fail or unknown summaries, not coordinates, tile flags, counts or city names. Matching reads alone do not prove atomicity or origin; establish a stable snapshot boundary and independently verify provenance.
- Investigate native eligibility and its ordering for already-owned, adjacent and nonadjacent tiles, the maximum area count and the relevant milestone. An eligibility preflight is not established as read-only: in the inspected reference it can initialize a zero maximum and invoke extension callbacks, and a later unlock invokes the native eligibility path again. An extension can change the computed adjacency Boolean after earlier checks. Check approved compatibility cases with other extensions and patches; a global milestone override is not an area-only solution.
- The inspected `IAreas.maxAreaCount = 25` setter is itself a game write and may dispatch milestone updates. Its timing, listeners, other-mod effects and failure behavior require separate design and explicit write authorization; merely observing the current maximum cannot prove that setting it is safe.
- Before proposing a scoped milestone intervention, prove exact call-site and nested-invocation identity, version compatibility, exception unwinding and behavior with other patches. A supplied Boolean, coordinates or claimed thread cannot exclude an unrelated nested call. No intervention is currently enabled.

## Evidence that read-only work cannot establish

- The `IAreas.UnlockArea(x, z, false)` call is void and may queue; it does not confirm success, establish unchanged funds, or prove causation. The inspected concrete `GameAreaManager.UnlockArea(index)` has a synchronous Boolean result, but `false` or an exception does not prove complete rollback and `true` does not prove same-city causation, no debit or persistence. Future native submission would need one authenticated adjacent candidate at a time, a confirmed newer same-city observation, and terminal handling for unknown or conflicting outcomes without automatic retry.
- Testing no debit, area-count and zone changes, terrain-patch failures, achievement effects, partial rollback, save/reload and compatibility requires a separately designed and expressly authorized controlled game-write phase. Funds and individual grid values must not be placed in the lifecycle diagnostic. A fail, crash, stale identity, unexpected side effect or uncertain outcome must stop the experiment; preserve the test state rather than cleaning or retrying automatically.
- The inspected native path can update zones and trigger an achievement before a later terrain result; a restored grid alone does not prove rollback. Any proposed handling of Steam, achievements or real save files needs its own scope, protection and authorization.

Only after these boundaries are supported by an implementable design, appropriate synthetic and reference checks, and separately authorized game evidence should Main consider a production adapter. Read-only observation by itself cannot establish safe writes, no debit or save compatibility.
