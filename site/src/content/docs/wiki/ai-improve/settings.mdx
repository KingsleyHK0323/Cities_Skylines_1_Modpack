For the public [Development source at `75ef944`](https://github.com/KingsleyHK0323/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/blob/75ef94407962817051cef4aa74ec6fbc1831fd02/SettingsPageUI.cs), open AI Improve's full settings from its **Options** page in Content Manager. The optional UnifiedUI in-game panel offers empty intercity train and bus scans and a settings reset; it does not open the full settings page. See [Using AI Improve in game](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-improve/in-game/).

The reviewed source defines 25 feature switches and one tuning-only card. The data below is bundled with the site, and its source commit and extraction date appear above the cards. This is static source review, not in-game acceptance; the stable Workshop interface and feature count have not been independently verified.

## Page layout

- **Header** — the mod's status and version, a **Changelog** button, a **Search settings** box and **Reset to defaults**.
- **General** — language, verbose logging and *Show advanced tuning values*.
- **One section per area** — Citizens, Emergency, Sanitation, Road Traffic, Rail, Aviation, Shipping & Cargo, Advanced and Experimental. Most feature cards have a switch; the Advanced tuning card has numeric controls only and appears when advanced tuning is enabled.
- **About** — links to the source code, this guide, the Steam Workshop page and the issue forms.

## General settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Language | Uses the game's language by default. Pick another to override it; the page redraws immediately. |
| Verbose logging | Enables some per-vehicle and per-event diagnostic messages. It may increase log output and performance costs. Off by default. |
| Show advanced tuning values | Reveals available numeric controls and the Advanced section. Hidden by default; changing values can stop a feature working without any visible sign. |

**Reset to defaults** restores every switch and value to a fresh install. Your language choice is kept.

## Search

When **Search settings** is available, enter a term and press Enter or move focus away to apply the filter across sections. Clear and submit the field, or use the back control, to return to the section list. The search field may be omitted when the options page is too narrow.
