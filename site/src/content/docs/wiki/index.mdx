## The mods

| Mod | Status | What it does today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AI Improve](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-improve/overview/) | Workshop release recorded · current availability not rechecked | In the public [Development source at `75ef944`](https://github.com/KingsleyHK0323/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/blob/75ef94407962817051cef4aa74ec6fbc1831fd02/SettingsPageUI.cs), vehicle, service and citizen behavior is intended to respond to congestion, free capacity and nearby needs; the source statically contains 25 switches. In-game acceptance is pending, and the stable Workshop count was not independently verified. |
| [Automated Public Transport Planning](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/transport/overview/) | Development prototype · game acceptance pending | Previews proposals and can attempt confirmed route creation; it may change saves. |
| [AI Gaming](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-gaming/overview/) | Development paused · earlier preview, runtime unverified | Earlier Development packaging paired a Windows x64 Companion with player-confirmed Ollama Local advice; current binary availability is unverified. |

Independent operation and compatibility are design goals; combined-mod acceptance has not been established.

## Find your way

- **New here?** [Getting started](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/getting-started/) covers subscribing, dependencies and choosing a version.
- **Using AI Improve?** The [feature](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-improve/features/) and [settings](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-improve/settings/) guides use data from public Development source `75ef944`; the [in-game guide](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-improve/in-game/) describes that source's UnifiedUI panel. The overview remains an earlier general description; installed versions may differ and in-game behavior has not been verified.
- **Something wrong?** The [FAQ](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-improve/faq/) explains how to collect a useful bug report.
- **What is next?** The [roadmap](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/project/roadmap/) shows what each mod is working towards.
